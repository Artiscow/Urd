# ADR-0016: The panel language and the element menu

Date: 8 August 2026. Status: accepted (v0.6, the panel-language rollout 0.6.6.6); decision 3 amended 3 October 2026 and 30 October 2026 (the narrow menu's form: the areas under each other in place of tabs), decisions 1 and 4 amended 7 November 2026 (the row kit: shared components held by a test, in place of shared classes), earlier texts in [amended/](0016-panel-language-until-2026-10-11.md), [amended/](0016-panel-language-until-2026-10-30.md) and [amended/](0016-panel-language-until-2026-11-07.md). The parts of the row kit are described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#the-editor-editor--templateadminassets).

## Context

Admin's panels (Pages, Theme, Nav, Footer, Collections, Plugins, Updates) plus Properties grew organically, and each panel invented its own recipes for rows, labels, chips and previews. The rebuild of the Theme panel piloted a coherent panel language: compact control rows, collapsible groups, segment buttons, small uppercase labels, pill chips and live previews. The Updates panel reused the idiom, but had to copy the recipes because they were not shared classes. Without a pinned contract the panels drift apart again.

Shared classes alone did not hold. By 7 November 2026 the element menu, the panels and the canvas chrome had drifted apart: segments were drawn three ways, sliders three ways and colour rows six ways, folds came in two kinds of which only one said its value while closed, and every list editor was its own. A class can be copied and changed in place; a component that every surface draws its rows through cannot.

At the same time the field study ([ELEMENTS.md](../../comparison/ELEMENTS.md), part 1, configuration surfaces) shows that nearly all modern builders split the inspector into Content vs Style: Gutenberg (Settings/Styles), Elementor (Content/Style/Advanced), Bricks (Content/Style), Carrd (Main/Appearance), GrapesJS (Traits/Style Manager). Without such a split, content fields, appearance, placement and animation stand in one long list.

## Decision

1. **The panel language is a shared kit of rows.** Every row in the admin (the element menu, the panels, the dialogs and the settings window alike) comes from a shared component in `editor/src/lib/rows/`, one per kind of row, chosen on the Design canvas «Block Menus» on 7 November 2026: the fold that says its value while closed, with its dot and its reset; a switch for on and off; a segment for two or three short choices (`lib/Choice.svelte`, never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009); a dropdown for more choices or longer ones; a slider with its value; a number to type or step; a colour swatch that opens the colour picker; a picture row that opens the design picker; a list editor; a sub-heading; a row that opens another panel; the search field; the empty state and the error line; and a setting's own dot with its way back to the default. The empty state and the error line are the ONLY legitimate uses of a prose surface in a panel; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]). tests/panel-language.test.mjs holds the kit: outside `lib/rows/` it finds the hand-made rows the kit replaces.
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a preview surface (the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The element menu: Content, Style and Placement.** Two areas alone leave placement, motion and the narrow-screen behaviour at the foot of Style, and a block type with many drawn options (the calendar designs) grows Style past what one column carries. The element menu (the floating menu from the gear on a block, and the Properties panel, both drawn by the same snippet) is therefore built on these decisions:
   - **Three areas.** Content (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice), Style (how it looks: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, hover) and Placement (where it sits and how it behaves there: the narrow-screen fit, motion, pinning, the frame, layer and rotation, visibility). Placement is not an «Advanced» drawer: it holds the same settings for every block, in the same order. No «Advanced» area exists: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin area is worse than none.
   - **One menu in two widths.** Wide, the three areas stand side by side as columns; narrow, they stand under each other, each with a heading that sticks to the top of its area while the menu scrolls and counts the area's groups and the changed ones, so a setting is reached by scrolling and never by switching. The admin setting «Element menu» chooses the width a menu opens in (wide unless set otherwise, kept in the browser), and a button in the menu's head switches for the session. The Properties panel in the rail is always narrow. The floating menu never lies over the admin's own panels; where the wide menu does not fit beside them it is narrow whatever is chosen.
   - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected.
   - **A quick row.** The settings used most stand above the areas as a row of their own, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **Every element menu is built from the same parts.** A block type contributes its Content and Style groups; the frame, the Placement area, the quick row and the search are shared.
4. **Every surface moves to the kit, and nothing moves back.** The surfaces are moved over step by step (BACKLOG 0.7.30); a file not yet moved stands on the test's list with what it still holds, and the list only shrinks. A new panel or control is built from the kit from the start, and a row drawn by hand beside it is a review finding, not a variant. A widget whose inner controls are its own (the colour picker's sliders, the image editor's tool rows) is named in the test as such.

## Consequences

- The kit's parts carry one set of values; a deviation between two panels is precisely the drift the kit removes.
- A row that the kit has no part for is added to the kit first, then used.
- The element menu's areas, groups and settings require UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) (ADR-0012).
