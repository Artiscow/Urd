### ADR-0016: The panel language and the element menu

Date: 8 August 2026. Status: accepted (v0.6, the panel-language rollout 0.6.6.6); decision 3 amended 3 October 2026 and 30 October 2026 (the narrow menu's form: the areas under each other in place of tabs), decisions 1 and 4 amended 7 November 2026 (the row kit: shared components held by a test, in place of shared classes), decision 3 amended later on 7 November 2026 (the element menu's frame: Pinned rows in place of the quick row, two columns when wide, and the block's own editor and designs first in their areas) and again that day (a text has no Content in its menu, the wide menu stands in two plain columns where Pinned and the areas are cut once, a group of one setting is drawn alone, and a shared setting keeps one area on every block), decision 3 amended 8 November 2026 (a section's settings in the same form, the actions at the foot of Placement, and one name, Settings), decision 1 amended 9 November 2026 (no mark on a changed fold or setting), decision 3 amended later on 9 November 2026 (motion in Style, a text's alignment, font and size open at the top of Style, and the element menu's form, folds, extra settings and shortcuts chosen in Urd settings, ADR-0030), decisions 1 and 3 amended again that day (a switch is its own way back; The parts of the row kit are described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#the-editor-editor--templateadminassets).

#### Context

Admin's panels (Pages, Theme, Nav, Footer, Collections, Plugins, Updates) plus Properties grew organically, and each panel invented its own recipes for rows, labels, chips and previews. The rebuild of the Theme panel piloted a coherent panel language: compact control rows, collapsible groups, segment buttons, small uppercase labels, pill chips and live previews. The Updates panel reused the idiom, but had to copy the recipes because they were not shared classes. Without a pinned contract the panels drift apart again.

Shared classes alone did not hold. By 7 November 2026 the element menu, the panels and the canvas chrome had drifted apart: segments were drawn three ways, sliders three ways and colour rows six ways, folds came in two kinds of which only one said its value while closed, and every list editor was its own. A class can be copied and changed in place; a component that every surface draws its rows through cannot.

At the same time the field study ([ELEMENTS.md](../../comparison/ELEMENTS.md), part 1, configuration surfaces) shows that nearly all modern builders split the inspector into Content vs Style: Gutenberg (Settings/Styles), Elementor (Content/Style/Advanced), Bricks (Content/Style), Carrd (Main/Appearance), GrapesJS (Traits/Style Manager). Without such a split, content fields, appearance, placement and animation stand in one long list.

#### Decision

1. **The panel language is a shared kit of rows.** Every row in the admin (the element menu, the panels, the dialogs and the settings window alike) comes from a shared component in `editor/src/lib/rows/`, one per kind of row, chosen on the Design canvas «Block Menus» on 7 November 2026: the fold that says its value while closed, with its reset; a switch for on and off; a segment for two or three short choices (`lib/Choice.svelte`, never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009); a dropdown for more choices or longer ones; a slider with its value; a number to type or step; a colour swatch that opens the colour picker; a picture row that opens the design picker; a list editor; a sub-heading; a row that opens another panel; the search field; the empty state and the error line; and a setting's way back to the default, which a switch does not have, since it is its own way back, with no mark of its own, nor a count of changed groups in an area's heading: what was changed is told in the History panel, and a dot is kept for what needs filling in (the Legal panel's). The empty state and the error line are the ONLY legitimate uses of a prose surface in a panel; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]). tests/panel-language.test.mjs holds the kit: outside `lib/rows/` it finds the hand-made rows the kit replaces.
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a preview surface (the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The element menu: Content, Style and Placement.** Two areas alone leave placement, motion and the narrow-screen behaviour at the foot of Style, and a block type with many drawn options (the calendar designs) grows Style past what one column carries. The element menu (the floating menu from the gear on a block, and the Settings panel, both drawn by the same snippet) is therefore built on these decisions:
   - **Three areas.** Content (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice), Style (how it looks and moves: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, motion as the page scrolls to it and under the pointer) and Placement (where it sits and how it behaves there: the narrow-screen fit, pinning, the frame, layer and rotation, visibility). Placement is not an «Advanced» drawer: it holds the same settings for every block, in the same order. No «Advanced» area exists: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin area is worse than none.
   - **One menu in the form chosen.** Wide, Pinned (where it stands) and the areas stand in two columns in their order, each whole, cut once between the columns where they come out most even when a block's menu opens, and kept so while it stays open: no column stands empty while another runs long, and a group that opens grows its own column without moving anything across (W1 on the Design canvas «Block Menus», chosen 7 November 2026, with each part kept whole); narrow, they stand under each other, each with a heading that sticks to the top of its area while the menu scrolls and counts the area's groups, so a setting is reached by scrolling; with tabs, Pinned, where it stands, stands over Content, Style and Placement as tabs with one area in view, and a tab whose area holds nothing for the block is left out. The admin setting «Where the menu opens», a part of the admin designs ([ADR-0030](../0030-admin-designs.md)), chooses the form: beside the block in two columns (the default), in one column or with tabs, or docked in the panel column in one column or with tabs under a card naming the block, where the gear opens the Settings panel instead of a floating menu; a button in the floating menu's head switches between wide and the chosen form for the session. The Settings panel stands in one column, or with tabs where that form is docked. The floating menu never lies over the admin's own panels; where the wide menu does not fit beside them it is narrow whatever is chosen. An area with nothing in it is not drawn.
   - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected, unless «Folds» in Urd settings opens one at a time in an area or all of them; «Settings in a group» there can show a group's first setting and those with a value of their own alone in the full menu, the rest added from «Add a setting» at the group's foot, which takes an added one away again, back to its standard; a small window from the block's bar shows its whole group. A group whose every setting stands in Pinned is left out, so no group stands empty; a group that holds a single setting is drawn as that setting alone, with its own way back; and a group that only switches something on is drawn as its switch alone until it is on.
   - **The search field first, or Pinned.** What stands first, before the areas, is chosen by «Top of the menu» in Urd settings: the search field alone (the default), Pinned, Pinned's rows as tiles, or chips that jump to each area. Pinned holds the settings used most, drawn as the rows they are in their areas, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top; a pinned setting stands there instead of in its area, so no setting is drawn twice, and the pin beside it takes it back. Without Pinned no pin is drawn, and every setting stands in its area.
   - **The block's own editor first in Content, its designs first in Style.** The way into a block's own editor (the image editor) is the first row of Content. A text is written on the page itself, so a text block's menu and the terms of sale's have no Content: the whole text's font and size stand in Style, and a text block's alignment, font and size stand open at its top, in no group. A block with designs has its design as a picture row first in Style, under the one pin id `design` whatever the block, with its phone design beside it where it has one: the looks that open the design picker stand side by side as buttons of one size, two to a line, each with its picture, its name and the design chosen in it, and a reset on its corner while the look is the block's own.
   - **A shared setting keeps one area on every block.** What a press does (the lightbox), what a visitor agrees to before something loads («Ask before loading») and the way into the image editor stand in Content, and the fonts in Style, whatever the block. This holds where only one design shows the setting too (the contact's map design, the short quote's portrait): such a setting stands in the area every block keeps it in, not under the design row.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **A section's settings in the same form, and one name for them.** The gear on a section's bar, and «Settings» in its right-click menu, open the section's settings in the floating menu beside it, as a block's gear does: Style and Placement, with the same groups, search and widths; the Settings panel draws the same. The gear, the right-click item and the panel are all named Settings.
   - **The actions stand in the menu as well.** A block's duplicate, to the front, to the back (on the desktop, where blocks overlap) and delete stand in its menu's head, on a line of their own just under it where a narrow menu's head holds the search field, whatever the way to add a capability ([ADR-0031](../0031-capabilities-in-the-element-menu.md)) and beside its «+» where «Adding extras» puts it there, the delete in dark red and acting at one press. At the foot of Placement a section's menu offers duplicate, move up, move down and delete, and a section's deletion there, as on its bar and in the right-click menu, acts at its second press.
   - **Every element menu is built from the same parts.** A block type contributes its Content and Style groups; the frame, the Placement area, the motion at the foot of Style, Pinned, the design rows and the search are shared.
4. **Every surface moves to the kit, and nothing moves back.** The surfaces are moved over step by step (BACKLOG 0.7.30); a file not yet moved stands on the test's list with what it still holds, and the list only shrinks. A new panel or control is built from the kit from the start, and a row drawn by hand beside it is a review finding, not a variant. A widget whose inner controls are its own (the colour picker's sliders, the image editor's tool rows) is named in the test as such.

#### Consequences

- The kit's parts carry one set of values; a deviation between two panels is precisely the drift the kit removes.
- A row that the kit has no part for is added to the kit first, then used.
- The element menu's areas, groups and settings require UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) (ADR-0012).

1. **The panel language is a shared kit of rows.** Every row in the admin (the element menu, the panels, the dialogs and the settings window alike) comes from a shared component in `editor/src/lib/rows/`, one per kind of row, chosen on the Design canvas «Block Menus» on 7 November 2026: the fold that says its value while closed, with its reset; a switch for on and off; a segment for two or three short choices (`lib/Choice.svelte`, never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009); a dropdown for more choices or longer ones; a slider with its value; a number to type or step; a colour swatch that opens the colour picker; a picture row that opens the design picker; a list editor; a sub-heading; a row that opens another panel; the search field; the empty state and the error line; and a setting's way back to the default, which a switch does not have, since it is its own way back, with no mark of its own, nor a count of changed groups in an area's heading: what was changed is told in the History panel, and a dot is kept for what needs filling in (the Legal panel's). The empty state and the error line are the ONLY legitimate uses of a prose surface in a panel; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]). tests/panel-language.test.mjs holds the kit: outside `lib/rows/` it finds the hand-made rows the kit replaces.
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a preview surface (the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The element menu: Content, Style and Placement.** Two areas alone leave placement, motion and the narrow-screen behaviour at the foot of Style, and a block type with many drawn options (the calendar designs) grows Style past what one column carries. The element menu (the floating menu from the gear on a block, and the Settings panel, both drawn by the same snippet) is therefore built on these decisions:
   - **Three areas.** Content (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice), Style (how it looks and moves: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, motion as the page scrolls to it and under the pointer) and Placement (where it sits and how it behaves there: the narrow-screen fit, pinning, the frame, layer and rotation, visibility). Placement is not an «Advanced» drawer: it holds the same settings for every block, in the same order. No «Advanced» area exists: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin area is worse than none.
   - **One menu in the form chosen.** Wide, Pinned (where it stands) and the areas stand in two columns in their order, each whole, cut once between the columns where they come out most even when a block's menu opens, and kept so while it stays open: no column stands empty while another runs long, and a group that opens grows its own column without moving anything across (W1 on the Design canvas «Block Menus», chosen 7 November 2026, with each part kept whole); narrow, they stand under each other, each with a heading that sticks to the top of its area while the menu scrolls and counts the area's groups, so a setting is reached by scrolling; with tabs, Pinned, where it stands, stands over Content, Style and Placement as tabs with one area in view, and a tab whose area holds nothing for the block is left out. The admin setting «Element menu», a part of the admin designs ([ADR-0030](../0030-admin-designs.md)), chooses the form: beside the block in two columns (the default), in one column or with tabs, or docked in the panel column in one column or with tabs under a card naming the block, where the gear opens the Settings panel instead of a floating menu; a button in the floating menu's head switches between wide and the chosen form for the session. The Settings panel stands in one column, or with tabs where that form is docked. The floating menu never lies over the admin's own panels; where the wide menu does not fit beside them it is narrow whatever is chosen. An area with nothing in it is not drawn.
   - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected, unless «Folds» in Urd settings opens one at a time in an area or all of them; «Extra settings» there can show a group's first setting and those with a value of their own alone in the full menu, the rest added from «Add a setting» at the group's foot, which takes an added one away again, back to its standard; a small window from the block's bar shows its whole group. A group whose every setting stands in Pinned is left out, so no group stands empty; a group that holds a single setting is drawn as that setting alone, with its own way back; and a group that only switches something on is drawn as its switch alone until it is on.
   - **The search field first, or Pinned.** What stands first, before the areas, is chosen by «Shortcuts» in Urd settings: the search field alone (the default), Pinned, Pinned's rows as tiles, or chips that jump to each area. Pinned holds the settings used most, drawn as the rows they are in their areas, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top; a pinned setting stands there instead of in its area, so no setting is drawn twice, and the pin beside it takes it back. Without Pinned no pin is drawn, and every setting stands in its area.
   - **The block's own editor first in Content, its designs first in Style.** The way into a block's own editor (the image editor) is the first row of Content. A text is written on the page itself, so a text block's menu and the terms of sale's have no Content: the whole text's font and size stand in Style, and a text block's alignment, font and size stand open at its top, in no group. A block with designs has its design as a picture row first in Style, under the one pin id `design` whatever the block, with its phone design under it where it has one.
   - **A shared setting keeps one area on every block.** What a press does (the lightbox), what a visitor agrees to before something loads («Ask before loading») and the way into the image editor stand in Content, and the fonts in Style, whatever the block. This holds where only one design shows the setting too (the contact's map design, the short quote's portrait): such a setting stands in the area every block keeps it in, not under the design row.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **A section's settings in the same form, and one name for them.** The gear on a section's bar, and «Settings» in its right-click menu, open the section's settings in the floating menu beside it, as a block's gear does: Style and Placement, with the same groups, search and widths; the Settings panel draws the same. The gear, the right-click item and the panel are all named Settings.
   - **The actions stand in the menu as well.** A block's duplicate, to the front, to the back (on the desktop, where blocks overlap) and delete stand in its menu's head, on a line of their own just under it where a narrow menu's head holds the search field, whatever the way to add a capability ([ADR-0031](../0031-capabilities-in-the-element-menu.md)) and beside its «+» where «Extra settings style» puts it there, the delete in dark red and acting at one press. At the foot of Placement a section's menu offers duplicate, move up, move down and delete, and a section's deletion there, as on its bar and in the right-click menu, acts at its second press.
   - **Every element menu is built from the same parts.** A block type contributes its Content and Style groups; the frame, the Placement area, the motion at the foot of Style, Pinned, the design rows and the search are shared.
4. **Every surface moves to the kit, and nothing moves back.** The surfaces are moved over step by step (BACKLOG 0.7.30); a file not yet moved stands on the test's list with what it still holds, and the list only shrinks. A new panel or control is built from the kit from the start, and a row drawn by hand beside it is a review finding, not a variant. A widget whose inner controls are its own (the colour picker's sliders, the image editor's tool rows) is named in the test as such.

#### Consequences

- The kit's parts carry one set of values; a deviation between two panels is precisely the drift the kit removes.
- A row that the kit has no part for is added to the kit first, then used.
- The element menu's areas, groups and settings require UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) (ADR-0012).

### ADR-0016: The panel language and the element menu

Date: 8 August 2026. Status: accepted (v0.6, the panel-language rollout 0.6.6.6); decision 3 amended 3 October 2026 and 30 October 2026 (the narrow menu's form: the areas under each other in place of tabs), decisions 1 and 4 amended 7 November 2026 (the row kit: shared components held by a test, in place of shared classes), decision 3 amended later on 7 November 2026 (the element menu's frame: Pinned rows in place of the quick row, two columns when wide, and the block's own editor and designs first in their areas) and again that day (a text has no Content in its menu, the wide menu stands in two plain columns where Pinned and the areas are cut once, a group of one setting is drawn alone, and a shared setting keeps one area on every block), decision 3 amended 8 November 2026 (a section's settings in the same form, the actions at the foot of Placement, and one name, Settings), decision 1 amended 9 November 2026 (no mark on a changed fold or setting), decision 3 amended later on 9 November 2026 (motion in Style, a text's alignment, font and size open at the top of Style, and the element menu's form, folds, extra settings and shortcuts chosen in Urd settings, ADR-0030), earlier texts in [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-10-11), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-10-30), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07-2), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07-3), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-08), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-09) and [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-09-2). The parts of the row kit are described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#the-editor-editor--templateadminassets).

#### Context

Admin's panels (Pages, Theme, Nav, Footer, Collections, Plugins, Updates) plus Properties grew organically, and each panel invented its own recipes for rows, labels, chips and previews. The rebuild of the Theme panel piloted a coherent panel language: compact control rows, collapsible groups, segment buttons, small uppercase labels, pill chips and live previews. The Updates panel reused the idiom, but had to copy the recipes because they were not shared classes. Without a pinned contract the panels drift apart again.

Shared classes alone did not hold. By 7 November 2026 the element menu, the panels and the canvas chrome had drifted apart: segments were drawn three ways, sliders three ways and colour rows six ways, folds came in two kinds of which only one said its value while closed, and every list editor was its own. A class can be copied and changed in place; a component that every surface draws its rows through cannot.

At the same time the field study ([ELEMENTS.md](../../comparison/ELEMENTS.md), part 1, configuration surfaces) shows that nearly all modern builders split the inspector into Content vs Style: Gutenberg (Settings/Styles), Elementor (Content/Style/Advanced), Bricks (Content/Style), Carrd (Main/Appearance), GrapesJS (Traits/Style Manager). Without such a split, content fields, appearance, placement and animation stand in one long list.

#### Decision

1. **The panel language is a shared kit of rows.** Every row in the admin (the element menu, the panels, the dialogs and the settings window alike) comes from a shared component in `editor/src/lib/rows/`, one per kind of row, chosen on the Design canvas «Block Menus» on 7 November 2026: the fold that says its value while closed, with its reset; a switch for on and off; a segment for two or three short choices (`lib/Choice.svelte`, never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009); a dropdown for more choices or longer ones; a slider with its value; a number to type or step; a colour swatch that opens the colour picker; a picture row that opens the design picker; a list editor; a sub-heading; a row that opens another panel; the search field; the empty state and the error line; and a setting's way back to the default, with no mark of its own, nor a count of changed groups in an area's heading: what was changed is told in the History panel, and a dot is kept for what needs filling in (the Legal panel's). The empty state and the error line are the ONLY legitimate uses of a prose surface in a panel; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]). tests/panel-language.test.mjs holds the kit: outside `lib/rows/` it finds the hand-made rows the kit replaces.
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a preview surface (the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The element menu: Content, Style and Placement.** Two areas alone leave placement, motion and the narrow-screen behaviour at the foot of Style, and a block type with many drawn options (the calendar designs) grows Style past what one column carries. The element menu (the floating menu from the gear on a block, and the Settings panel, both drawn by the same snippet) is therefore built on these decisions:
   - **Three areas.** Content (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice), Style (how it looks and moves: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, motion as the page scrolls to it and under the pointer) and Placement (where it sits and how it behaves there: the narrow-screen fit, pinning, the frame, layer and rotation, visibility). Placement is not an «Advanced» drawer: it holds the same settings for every block, in the same order. No «Advanced» area exists: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin area is worse than none.
   - **One menu in the form chosen.** Wide, Pinned and the areas stand in two columns in their order, each whole, cut once between the columns where they come out most even when a block's menu opens, and kept so while it stays open: no column stands empty while another runs long, and a group that opens grows its own column without moving anything across (W1 on the Design canvas «Block Menus», chosen 7 November 2026, with each part kept whole); narrow, they stand under each other, each with a heading that sticks to the top of its area while the menu scrolls and counts the area's groups, so a setting is reached by scrolling; with tabs, Pinned stands over Content, Style and Placement as tabs with one area in view, and a tab whose area holds nothing for the block is left out. The admin setting «Element menu», a part of the admin designs ([ADR-0030](../0030-admin-designs.md)), chooses the form: beside the block in two columns (the default), in one column or with tabs, or docked in the panel column in one column or with tabs under a card naming the block, where the gear opens the Settings panel instead of a floating menu; a button in the floating menu's head switches between wide and the chosen form for the session. The Settings panel stands in one column, or with tabs where that form is docked. The floating menu never lies over the admin's own panels; where the wide menu does not fit beside them it is narrow whatever is chosen. An area with nothing in it is not drawn.
   - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected, unless «Folds» in Urd settings opens one at a time in an area or all of them; «Extra settings» there can show a group's first setting and those with a value of their own alone, the rest added from a ⋮ at its top. A group whose every setting stands in Pinned is left out, so no group stands empty, and a group that holds a single setting is drawn as that setting alone, still marked and reset as the block's own.
   - **Pinned.** The settings used most stand first, before the areas, as Pinned, drawn as the rows they are in their areas, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top. A pinned setting stands there instead of in its area, so no setting is drawn twice, and the pin beside it takes it back. «Shortcuts» in Urd settings draws the same rows as tiles, or puts the search field alone or chips that jump to each area first instead.
   - **The block's own editor first in Content, its designs first in Style.** The way into a block's own editor (the image editor) is the first row of Content. A text is written on the page itself, so a text block's menu and the terms of sale's have no Content: the whole text's font and size stand in Style, and a text block's alignment, font and size stand open at its top, in no group. A block with designs has its design as a picture row first in Style, under the one pin id `design` whatever the block, with its phone design under it where it has one.
   - **A shared setting keeps one area on every block.** What a press does (the lightbox), what a visitor agrees to before something loads («Ask before loading») and the way into the image editor stand in Content, and the fonts in Style, whatever the block. This holds where only one design shows the setting too (the contact's map design, the short quote's portrait): such a setting stands in the area every block keeps it in, not under the design row.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **A section's settings in the same form, and one name for them.** The gear on a section's bar, and «Settings» in its right-click menu, open the section's settings in the floating menu beside it, as a block's gear does: Style and Placement, with the same groups, search and widths; the Settings panel draws the same. A deletion there, as on a section's bar and in the right-click menu, acts at its second press.

4. **Every surface moves to the kit, and nothing moves back.** The surfaces are moved over step by step (BACKLOG 0.7.30); a file not yet moved stands on the test's list with what it still holds, and the list only shrinks.    - **The block's own editor first in Content, its designs first in Style.** The way into a block's own editor (the image editor) is the first row of Content. A text is written on the page itself, so a text block's menu and the terms of sale's have no Content: the whole text's font and size stand in Style. A block with designs has its design as a picture row first in Style, under the one pin id `design` whatever the block, with its phone design under it where it has one.
   - **A shared setting keeps one area on every block.** What a press does (the lightbox), what a visitor agrees to before something loads («Ask before loading») and the way into the image editor stand in Content, and the fonts in Style, whatever the block. This holds where only one design shows the setting too (the contact's map design, the short quote's portrait): such a setting stands in the area every block keeps it in, not under the design row.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **A section's settings in the same form, and one name for them.** The gear on a section's bar, and «Settings» in its right-click menu, open the section's settings in the floating menu beside it, as a block's gear does: Style and Placement, with the same groups, search and widths; the Settings panel draws the same. The gear, the right-click item and the panel are all named Settings.
   - **The actions stand in the menu as well.** At the foot of Placement a block's menu offers duplicate, to the front, to the back (on the desktop, where blocks overlap) and delete, and a section's offers duplicate, move up, move down and delete. A deletion there, as on a section's bar and in the right-click menu, acts at its second press.
   - **Every element menu is built from the same parts.** A block type contributes its Content and Style groups; the frame, the Placement area, Pinned, the design rows and the search are shared.
4. **Every surface moves to the kit, and nothing moves back.** The surfaces are moved over step by step (BACKLOG 0.7.30); a file not yet moved stands on the test's list with what it still holds, and the list only shrinks. A new panel or control is built from the kit from the start, and a row drawn by hand beside it is a review finding, not a variant. A widget whose inner controls are its own (the colour picker's sliders, the image editor's tool rows) is named in the test as such.

#### Consequences

- The kit's parts carry one set of values; a deviation between two panels is precisely the drift the kit removes.
- A row that the kit has no part for is added to the kit first, then used.
- The element menu's areas, groups and settings require UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) (ADR-0012).

### ADR-0016: The panel language and the element menu

Date: 8 August 2026. Status: accepted (v0.6, the panel-language rollout 0.6.6.6); decision 3 amended 3 October 2026 and 30 October 2026 (the narrow menu's form: the areas under each other in place of tabs), decisions 1 and 4 amended 7 November 2026 (the row kit: shared components held by a test, in place of shared classes), decision 3 amended later on 7 November 2026 (the element menu's frame: Pinned rows in place of the quick row, two columns when wide, and the block's own editor and designs first in their areas) and again that day (a text has no Content in its menu, the wide menu stands in two plain columns where Pinned and the areas are cut once, a group of one setting is drawn alone, and a shared setting keeps one area on every block), earlier texts in [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-10-11), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-10-30), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07), [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07-2) and [amended/](0016-panel-language.md#until-2026-11-07-3). The parts of the row kit are described in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../../ARCHITECTURE.md#the-editor-editor--templateadminassets).

#### Context

Admin's panels (Pages, Theme, Nav, Footer, Collections, Plugins, Updates) plus Properties grew organically, and each panel invented its own recipes for rows, labels, chips and previews. The rebuild of the Theme panel piloted a coherent panel language: compact control rows, collapsible groups, segment buttons, small uppercase labels, pill chips and live previews. The Updates panel reused the idiom, but had to copy the recipes because they were not shared classes. Without a pinned contract the panels drift apart again.

Shared classes alone did not hold. By 7 November 2026 the element menu, the panels and the canvas chrome had drifted apart: segments were drawn three ways, sliders three ways and colour rows six ways, folds came in two kinds of which only one said its value while closed, and every list editor was its own. A class can be copied and changed in place; a component that every surface draws its rows through cannot.

At the same time the field study ([ELEMENTS.md](../../comparison/ELEMENTS.md), part 1, configuration surfaces) shows that nearly all modern builders split the inspector into Content vs Style: Gutenberg (Settings/Styles), Elementor (Content/Style/Advanced), Bricks (Content/Style), Carrd (Main/Appearance), GrapesJS (Traits/Style Manager). Without such a split, content fields, appearance, placement and animation stand in one long list.

#### Decision

1. **The panel language is a shared kit of rows.** Every row in the admin (the element menu, the panels, the dialogs and the settings window alike) comes from a shared component in `editor/src/lib/rows/`, one per kind of row, chosen on the Design canvas «Block Menus» on 7 November 2026: the fold that says its value while closed, with its dot and its reset; a switch for on and off; a segment for two or three short choices (`lib/Choice.svelte`, never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009); a dropdown for more choices or longer ones; a slider with its value; a number to type or step; a colour swatch that opens the colour picker; a picture row that opens the design picker; a list editor; a sub-heading; a row that opens another panel; the search field; the empty state and the error line; and a setting's own dot with its way back to the default. The empty state and the error line are the ONLY legitimate uses of a prose surface in a panel; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]). tests/panel-language.test.mjs holds the kit: outside `lib/rows/` it finds the hand-made rows the kit replaces.
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a preview surface (the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The element menu: Content, Style and Placement.** Two areas alone leave placement, motion and the narrow-screen behaviour at the foot of Style, and a block type with many drawn options (the calendar designs) grows Style past what one column carries. The element menu (the floating menu from the gear on a block, and the Properties panel, both drawn by the same snippet) is therefore built on these decisions:
   - **Three areas.** Content (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice), Style (how it looks: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, hover) and Placement (where it sits and how it behaves there: the narrow-screen fit, motion, pinning, the frame, layer and rotation, visibility). Placement is not an «Advanced» drawer: it holds the same settings for every block, in the same order. No «Advanced» area exists: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin area is worse than none.
   - **One menu in two widths.** Wide, Pinned and the areas stand in two columns in their order, each whole, cut once between the columns where they come out most even when a block's menu opens, and kept so while it stays open: no column stands empty while another runs long, and a group that opens grows its own column without moving anything across (W1 on the Design canvas «Block Menus», chosen 7 November 2026, with each part kept whole); narrow, they stand under each other, each with a heading that sticks to the top of its area while the menu scrolls and counts the area's groups and the changed ones, so a setting is reached by scrolling and never by switching. The admin setting «Element menu» chooses the width a menu opens in (wide unless set otherwise, kept in the browser), and a button in the menu's head switches for the session. The Properties panel in the rail is always narrow. The floating menu never lies over the admin's own panels; where the wide menu does not fit beside them it is narrow whatever is chosen. An area with nothing in it is not drawn.
   - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected. A group whose every setting stands in Pinned is left out, so no group stands empty, and a group that holds a single setting is drawn as that setting alone, still marked and reset as the block's own.
   - **Pinned.** The settings used most stand first, before the areas, as Pinned, drawn as the rows they are in their areas, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top.    - **Collapsible groups that show their value.** Inside an area the settings stand in collapsible groups (decision 1's building block) whose summary carries the group's current value while closed, so the menu can be read without opening anything. A group stays open or closed as it was left when another block is selected.
   - **A quick row.** The settings used most stand above the areas as a row of their own, with a default set per block type and the site owner's own pins on top.
   - **A picker takes the whole menu.** A choice among many drawn options (the calendar's designs) opens over the areas at the menu's full width and returns to them with one button, instead of unfolding inside a column.
   - **Every element menu is built from the same parts.** A block type contributes its Content and Style groups; the frame, the Placement area, the quick row and the search are shared.
4. **The rollout is additive.** Panels are moved over to the shared classes panel by panel, and new panels and new controls use them from the start. A new class that duplicates a building block with drifted values is a review finding, not a variant.

#### Consequences

- The shared classes carry one set of values; small pixel deviations between the panels are precisely the drift the contract removes.
- The element menu's areas, groups and settings require UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) (ADR-0012).

### ADR-0016: The panel language and the Content/Style model

Date: 8 August 2026. Status: accepted (v0.6, the panel-language rollout 0.6.6.6).

#### Context

Admin's six panels (Pages, Theme, Nav, Footer, Collections, Plugins) plus Properties grew organically, and each panel invented its own recipes for rows, labels, chips and previews. During the rebuild of the Theme panel (0.6.6.5.4) a coherent panel language was piloted: compact control rows, collapsible groups, segment buttons, small uppercase labels, pill chips and live previews. The Updates panel (0.6.10) reused the idiom, but had to copy the CSS recipes because they were not shared classes: three nearly identical row recipes (`.autorow`, `.palhead`, `.update-opt-head`), three nearly identical uppercase labels and two nearly identical chips arose over two rounds. Without a pinned contract the panels drift apart again.

At the same time the field study ([ELEMENTKART.md](../../comparison/README.md#former-numbers) parts 1.2 and 9) shows that nearly all modern builders split the inspector into Content vs Style: Gutenberg (Settings/Styles), Elementor (Content/Style/Advanced), Bricks (Content/Style), Carrd (Main/Appearance), GrapesJS (Traits/Style Manager). Urd's Properties panel has no such split today: content fields, appearance, placement and animation stand in one long list.

#### Decision

1. **The panel language is a shared contract.** The building blocks live as shared CSS classes in the editor's panel style, and panels compose them instead of writing their own recipes:
   - **Panel heading:** `.panel-strong` for section titles in a panel.
   - **Control row:** `.ctl-row` (flex, label left / control right). Toggle rows use `.gridmenu-snap` (text left, toggle at the far right), value readouts use `.gridmenu-value` (tabular-nums).
   - **Collapsible group:** `<details class="group">` with `.group-items` inside; an open group is marked with an accent border. Section folders without a pill frame use the `.frame-group` variant.
   - **Segment control:** `.seg` with the `.on` state for the active choice (never a native `<select>`, ADR-0009).
   - **Mini label:** `.mini-label` (small uppercase label with letter-spacing) for column and group markers.
   - **Chip:** `.chip` (999px pill in currentColor, muted) with the `.accent` variant for the active/highlighted choice.
   - **Preview surface:** `.sample` (muted surface with a thin border and radius) as the base for live samples.
   - **Empty state and errors:** `.panel-hint` (with the `.place-error` pattern for errors). These are the ONLY legitimate uses; explanatory prose belongs in «?» tooltips or the help chip (the prose rule in [AGENTS.md]).
2. **Live previews.** Settings that change appearance shall show the effect where they are set: a sample on a `.sample` surface (pilot: the palette preview, the typography sample and the corner sample in Theme) or directly in the control itself. A setting the user has to «save and check» is a deviation.
3. **The Content/Style model.** The Properties panel is split into TWO segment tabs at the top: **Content** (what the block says and shows: text and content fields, plugin fields via the field contract, collection binding, media choice) and **Style** (how it looks and moves: colours, background, borders, typography overrides, hover, animation, plus placement/layer/rotation). NO third «Advanced» tab is introduced: Urd does not have the depth (custom CSS, responsive overrides per field, motion conditions) that justifies Elementor's three-way split, and an empty or thin tab is worse than none. The distribution of each individual setting is decided in the rollout round for Properties; the model (two tabs, the definitions above) is pinned here.
4. **The rollout is additive and round by round.** The panels are moved over to the shared classes in the backlog rounds under 0.6.6.6; new panels and new controls SHALL use them from the start. A new class that duplicates a building block with drifted values is a review finding, not a variant.

#### Consequences

- The foundation round consolidates the pilot's near-duplicates: `.autorow`/`.palhead`/`.update-opt-head` become `.ctl-row` (plus any context class carrying only margins), `.palname`/`.tpv-cap`/`.update-opt-label` become `.mini-label`, `.stdtag`/`.update-tag` become `.chip`/`.chip.accent`, and the surface recipe in `.typo-sample`/`.form-prev` becomes `.sample`. The values are unified; small pixel deviations between the panels are precisely the drift being removed.
- The panel-hint prose that breaks the prose rule (33 paragraphs, its own backlog item) is migrated to tooltips/the help chip in step with the rollout rounds, panel by panel.
- The Properties tabs require new UI keys in the core languages (nb, en-GB, tr) when they are built (ADR-0012).
- The markup changes live in `editor/src/App.svelte`; each rollout round rebuilds and commits the bundle as usual.
